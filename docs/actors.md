# Actors

An Actor is a character sheet that describes a person in your adventures. Actors have a [name](#name), [description](#description), [set of expressions](#expressions), and most importantly, an [ID](#id).

## Actor Properties

### Name

The Actor's name. i.e, *John Doe* or *Mary Sue*.

### Description

An in-depth text that describes who or what the actor is. You can format it however you want.

### ID

All actors have their own unique ID. Think of it like a username or handle - it is used by the AI to tell different actors apart and to refer to them when creating dialogue or taking actions.
