# Actors

An Actor is a character sheet that describes a character in your adventures. Actors have a [name](#name), [description](#description), [set of expressions](#expressions), and most importantly, an [ID](#id).

## Table of Contents

- [ID](#id)
- [Name](#name)
- [Description](#description)
- [Expressions](#expressions)



## Actor Properties

### ID

Every Actor needs a unique ID. Think of it like a username or handle that the AI uses to reference actors when making them speak, or when taking action; removing/adding actors to the scene, changing their health, etc. *If this field is left empty, the engine will treat the actor's name as the ID.*

### Name

The Actor's name. i.e, *John Doe* or *Mary Sue*.

### Description

An in-depth text that describes who or what the actor is. You can format it however you want.
