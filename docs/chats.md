# Chats

A chat is a conversation between the player (you) and the AI. This is where adventures happen. The AI can be given awareness of the world and its characters by configuring a [list of actors](#actor-list) and a [list of notes](#notes). Every time you send a message, the AI will take action and move the story forward.

## Actor List

In order for the AI to be aware of and interact with characters, you need to add [actors](actors.md) to the chat. Find the actor list in the right sidebar and click the plus icon to the right of the "Actors" header. This opens a menu where you can select actors to add to the chat.

## Note List

You can attach notes to a chat to customize that AI's behavior. To learn more about notes, [click here](notes.md). The note list is located in the right sidebar, below the actor list.
